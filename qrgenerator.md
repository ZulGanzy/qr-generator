  - Download QR code at multiple quality scales (1x, 2x, 4x, 8x)
  - Configurable download margin
  - Save QR codes to localStorage
  - Preview saved QR codes in a modal
  - Responsive dark-themed UI
  - Persistent styling configuration across sessions
